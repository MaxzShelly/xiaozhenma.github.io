    Irvine, California, USA

    Project Lead — PIKAN-Drive: Progressive Physics-Informed KANs from PilotNet Steering Regression to Fast-BEV 3D Detection
    University of California, Irvine
    Led a progressive study of MLP, KAN, and PI-KAN architectures from PilotNet steering regression to Fast-BEV multi-camera 3D detection, establishing unified training, evaluation, and ablation pipelines.
    • Built a PilotNet benchmark on 45,406 sequential Udacity samples; the best PI-KAN achieved MAE 3.209 and RMSE 6.163, reducing error by 11.3% and 9.7% relative to the MLP baseline.
    • Improved the Fast-BEV KAN baseline from 0.2559 to 0.2720 mAP and from 0.3950 to 0.4046 NDS.
    • Designed nine grouped physics-informed auxiliary losses and conducted targeted Fast-BEV ablations; additional constraints did not consistently improve detection, motivating task-specific, metric-aligned, and architecture-aware supervision.
    • Supervised by Prof. Fadi Kurdahi.

    State Key Laboratory of Rail Transit Vehicle System, Southwest Jiaotong University
    Developed the FrustumDense-SAM-XC3D railway-scene reconstruction system using multi-frame LiDAR, RGB images, and monocular depth.
    • Integrated point-cloud densification, depth fusion, object segmentation, occlusion handling, and scene visualisation into a single pipeline.

  • 2024 - 2025

    Chengdu, China

    Project Lead — Machine Learning-Driven 3D Reconstruction of High-Speed Train Head Shapes
    State Key Laboratory of Rail Transit Vehicle System, Southwest Jiaotong University
    Investigated robust reconstruction of high-speed train heads from sparse point-cloud observations.
    • Constructed a portable multi-sensor acquisition device with LiDAR, cameras, and IMUs; developed a radar-vision tightly coupled SLAM framework based on FAST-LIVO2 with extended Kalman filtering for camera-pose optimisation.
    • Improved registration with enhanced ICP and dynamic-threshold denoising; generated uniform, high-precision point clouds through normal-vector estimation and resampling.
    • Introduced an incremental k-d tree for map storage and retrieval, plus motion-consistency-based dynamic-object exclusion for localisation robustness.
    • Proposed tri-axial projection segmentation interpolation and reverse hole-boundary identification, raising model integrity from 75% to 98%.
    • Explored Greedy triangulation, Poisson reconstruction, B-spline interpolation, Moving Cuboid, and radial-basis-function interpolation for complex surface modelling.
    • Independently authored the technical report Three-Dimensional Surface Reconstruction of High-Speed Train Noses from Sparse Point Clouds.
    • Supervised by Prof. Zhao Tang.
